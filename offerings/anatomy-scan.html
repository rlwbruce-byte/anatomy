Anatomy Scan.

What it is

The Anatomy Scan is a two-week engagement that maps your go-to-market organization as it actually operates today, aligns your team on the foundations underneath it, and scores the opportunities where AI would earn its keep.

It is diagnosis before prescription. We do not build anything during the Scan, and you are not committed to building anything after it. What you own at the end is a picture of the current state that your team agrees with, and a sequence for what to do about it.

Why it exists

Two challenges show up in nearly every company we talk to. Either the foundations are not written down, or they are written down and sales, marketing, and product each carry a different version.

Both produce the same result once AI is in the workflow: output that is faster, louder, and less differentiated than what came before it. AI does not create alignment. It scales whatever you feed it. The Scan is how we make sure what you feed it is right, and how we find out where AI belongs in your motion before anyone spends money building it.

What you get

Inside the engagement

  • A current-state map

    Your go-to-market organization as it operates today: team, motion, systems, data quality, and where AI is already in use, including the places nobody has told you about.

  • Aligned foundations

    Ideal customer profile (ICP), personas, messaging, differentiators, proof points, competitors, and buying signals, agreed across sales, marketing, and product and written in the words the team settled on.

  • 15 to 20 scored opportunities

    Ranked by impact, effort, and cost, so the argument for sequencing is on the page rather than in someone's head.

  • A 90-day roadmap

    With sequencing, owners, and the measurement attached to each move.

  • A written read on when and where AI makes sense

    In your motion specifically, including the places it does not.

  • A working session

    We walk your team through all of it, together, because a roadmap nobody has argued with is not aligned.

Why you need it

You are about to spend real money on AI. The question is not whether the tools work. It is whether the context underneath them is right, and whether the sequence is right.

A roadmap built on foundations your team has actually agreed to is the difference between infrastructure and expensive experimentation. If you go on to build, the Anatomy Scan fee credits into the build. If you do not, the roadmap is still yours, and you can run it with an internal team or another partner.

Proof points and ROI

What we hold ourselves to

  • Scoped to two weeks, so the decision it informs is not held up for a quarter.
  • Every opportunity is scored, so the roadmap is defensible to a board, an operating partner, or a finance review.
  • Measurement is attached at the roadmap stage: pipeline, cycle time, and cost per outcome, not added afterwards.
  • We decline "automate what we already do" briefs, and we hold the roadmap to the same standard.
