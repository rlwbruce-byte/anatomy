## What it is

Anatomy OS is the complete build: infrastructure and skills built for your business, plus enablement and training for your go-to-market (GTM) team to drive adoption. It starts with a two-week sprint that gets your leadership team aligned on the foundations, and ends with those foundations written into a system your team runs every day.

Anatomy OS is what we call it. What you are buying is an AI-native go-to-market operating system, tuned to your business, your brand, and your goals. It starts with the GTM team, because that is where the pressure is, and it is architected to scale across the rest of the organization, so every function that represents the brand is eventually working from the same context.

## In the room

### What we align your leadership team on.

Each daily session takes one foundation, tests it against what the intake surfaced, and leaves with a decision rather than a draft.

**Market position and competition**

Where you sit, who you are really up against, and where the market is moving.

**Ideal customer profile (ICP)**

Who you sell to, who you do not, and the signals that tell the two apart.

**Personas**

For each persona: the outcomes they want, the challenges in the way, and the key metrics they are held to.

**Messaging and positioning**

What you say, how you say it, and the differentiators every skill and agent will carry.

**Product portfolio**

How the offering fits together, and how each part is positioned for the buyer.

And the other foundations your intake surfaces. The list follows your business, not a template.
