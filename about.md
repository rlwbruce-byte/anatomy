## The foundations

### The anatomy of a revenue engine

The information that has always been required for a successful go-to-market organization is still required, at every size and every stage. We work through it in this order, because it is a climb and each step depends on the one below it.

1. Ideal customer profile (ICP)

2. Product category

3. Competitors, direct and indirect

4. Personas

5. Messaging

6. Differentiators

7. Proof points

8. Buying signals

9. Data quality

10. Customer relationship management (CRM)

11. Enrichment

12. Outbound process

13. Predictable pipeline

14. Then, and only then: can AI just do it?

Two challenges show up in nearly every company we talk to. They do not have these foundations written down, or they have them and have not agreed on them. Either way, the team is not working from the same source of information, and that is where undifferentiated AI-slop comes from.

The part that is ours is what happens after the alignment. The foundations do not just get agreed once. Your AI Operating System updates them for you and with you. If your brand has moved past the talking points still sitting on your website, we identify what the outside world sees, make the adjustments, and feed them back into the system. Every piece of content from that point forward is attuned to the new brand. The same system feeds audits and update projects, so you know when and where to fix your owned and earned properties, because in today's AI world, inconsistency gets counted against you.

## How we work

### Four things we hold to

**The foundation comes first**

The fundamentals that have always made go-to-market work are still required, at every size and stage. Most companies do not have them, or do not agree on them. We align the team on the foundations before AI touches anything, and we decline briefs that ask us to automate what already exists.

**Your context, not everyone's**

Everyone has the same models. Differentiation comes from your context. We build your AI Operating System from your own material, never a template, so the work you do once is used every time, by everyone on the team.

**A system that keeps learning**

Working files go stale the day after they are written. Your AI Operating System updates the foundations with you, identifies where the outside world has drifted from the brand, and feeds audits and update projects across your owned and earned properties.

**When and where AI makes sense**

Not every task is an AI use case. AI costs are rising and the environmental impact is real. We tell you where AI earns its keep, build it so it is cost-effective at scale, and hand you infrastructure you own and can trust. Part of what you get is someone who will say "this is not a use case for AI."
