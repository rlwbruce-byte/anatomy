# AEO Content Auditor

## What this skill does

Answer Engine Optimization is the SEO successor for a world where buyers
ask an LLM instead of typing a search query. Paste in a page, blog post, or
product doc and Claude evaluates it the way an answer engine would: is the
core claim extractable in one sentence, is it structured so a model can
cite it cleanly, and does it actually answer the question a buyer would
ask — versus talking about itself.

## Trigger phrases

- "Run an AEO audit on this page"
- "Would an AI answer engine cite this content?"
- "Rewrite this so it's more extractable for AI search"

## What you say

> "Here's our product page copy for [Product]. We want to show up when
> someone asks ChatGPT 'what's the best tool for quote-to-cash between
> Salesforce and NetSuite.'"

## What Claude delivers

- **Extractability score** — can the core claim be lifted as a clean,
  standalone answer, or is it buried in marketing language?
- **Question-fit check** — does the content actually answer the buyer
  question, or only describe the product?
- **Structure fixes** — specific rewrites (headers, definitional openers,
  comparison framing) that make the page easier for a model to summarize
  and cite accurately
- **Citation risk flags** — claims an answer engine would likely omit or
  soften because they're unverifiable or overly promotional

## Perfect for

- Auditing existing product or category pages before a content refresh
- Writing new comparison or "best X for Y" pages with AEO in mind from the start
- Checking whether competitor content is more likely to get cited than yours
- Briefing writers on the structural patterns answer engines favor

## Scope

Works from content you paste in or a URL's copy — it does not query live
answer engines to test actual citation behavior.

## Setup required

None — prompt-only skill.

## Works great with

- Competitor Intelligence Brief — see whether a competitor's content is
  winning the answer-engine citation battle in your category
