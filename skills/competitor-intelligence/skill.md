# Competitor Intelligence Brief

## What this skill does

Give Claude a competitor name (or a batch of them) and any raw signals you've
gathered — a changelog, a pricing page, a review site export, a LinkedIn post,
a call transcript mentioning them — and it produces a structured competitive
brief: what changed, why it matters, and how your team should respond.

Built for teams who need to keep sales current on 5-10+ competitors without a
dedicated competitive intelligence function.

## Trigger phrases

- "Build a competitor brief on [Competitor]"
- "What changed with [Competitor] this week?"
- "Turn this into a battlecard update"

## What you say

> "Competitor is Acme Corp. Here's their new pricing page and a G2 review
> from last week complaining about implementation time. We're mid-cycle
> with two prospects evaluating them."

## What Claude delivers

- **Headline change** — the single most important shift, in one sentence
- **What it means for deals in flight** — how this affects active
  opportunities where this competitor is in play
- **Battlecard-ready talking points** — 3-5 lines a rep can use verbatim
- **Watch items** — what to monitor next (renewal cycles, hiring signals,
  funding events)

## Perfect for

- Weekly or monthly competitive monitoring cadences
- Prepping a rep before a competitive deal review
- Feeding a living battlecard doc without manual upkeep
- Briefing leadership before a board update that touches competitive landscape

## Scope

Works from whatever source material you provide — it does not browse the
web on its own. Feed it screenshots, pasted text, PDFs, or call notes.

## Setup required

None — this is a prompt-only skill. No API keys or integrations needed to
get started; works best when paired with a recurring source of competitor
signals (a monitoring tool, a shared inbox, a Slack channel).

## Works great with

- ABM Account Snapshot — layer competitive context into account-level plans
- AEO Content Auditor — check whether a competitor is winning share of voice
  in AI answer engines
